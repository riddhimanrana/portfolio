By January, I'd reached out to more than 70 clubs, teachers, administrators, and local organizations about Let's Assist. A lot of that was sending follow-ups and trying to get a meeting during lunch or after school.

Ms. Erickson at WRMS tried it and asked for recurring events, calendar integration, and saved drafts. I built those, but getting district approval was slower. I spoke at the SRVUSD board meeting in October 2025, then worked through my CS teacher, counselor, and school administrators to reach IT.

![Speaking at the SRVUSD Board of Education meeting](./lets-assist-beyond-the-demo/board-meeting.png)

*My part of the [board recording starts at 1:29:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCHJ60q5PjY&t=5394s).*

In January, I met with Ms. Matsumoto and Mr. Gerbert about Interact and local events. I started building around their waivers and registration process. Then the Education Foundation chose an established system for Truck Time and Run for Education. I'd already spent time on the changes, so that was disappointing. We kept talking about other ways to try it.

Troop 941 gave me somewhere to use it for real. I presented it in February, and the troop started using it for signups, waivers, and reports.

![Presenting Let's Assist to Troop 941](./lets-assist-beyond-the-demo/troop-presentation.jpg)

*The [troop slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1lVeFxzUxJy5fYManfnKKaGm5ZL4MEiE0SFMuL2PEHp4/edit).*

Hearts4Hands tried an event too. The first trial had six signups, but we didn't get the link to CSF in time. Conversations with the PTA and the Knights of Columbus brought up different needs, including signing people up without making them create accounts.

CITE invited me to sign a student data privacy agreement in February. I met with SRVUSD's IT director in March and was referred to the data manager in May. The agreement wasn't signed.

Then, on March 27, a student emailed me about CSF officer applications. They closed in two hours. I asked which role would let me bring Let's Assist into the club, and she suggested webmaster or secretary.

CSF needed a lot beyond event signups. I built around membership, attendance, point evidence, officer review, and importing the existing spreadsheets. Connecting those records to accounts took ages. Similar names weren't enough to decide two records belonged to the same person.

![Introducing Let's Assist at the DVHS CSF meeting](./lets-assist-beyond-the-demo/csf-meeting.jpg)

By September, we were introducing Let's Assist for CSF posts, activities, and point submissions. It now has 900+ users. There's still work to do, but seeing it used at the CSF meeting was something I'd been trying to get to for a long time.
